### Scope and exam instructions

- **Explore:** Filter water, sand, and dissolved sugar (glucose) and interpret the glucose test strip.
- **Learning point 1:** Lungs remove carbon dioxide and water vapor.
- **Learning point 2:** Skin releases sweat and helps cool the body.
- **Learning point 3:** Two kidneys, two ureters, urinary bladder, urethra, and their functions.
- **Learning point 4:** Blood filtration, returning useful materials, forming and removing urine.
- **Learning point 5:** Healthy habits for lungs, skin, and kidneys.

Answer all **Student questions** before checking the separated **Answer key**. For correction questions, identify the error and rewrite the complete statement accurately. For true/false, explain how to fix false statements. Award full credit to scientifically equivalent, grade-appropriate free-response answers.

**Accuracy notes:** The classroom filter-paper activity is a **model**, not an exact copy of a living kidney; dissolved glucose passes through the paper, while real kidneys return needed substances to the blood. The urinary bladder **stores** urine but does not filter blood. Water is removed in more than one form (exhaled vapor, sweat, urine). Questions follow the supplied lesson and its terminology, not unrelated advanced physiology. These are original exam-style **practice predictions**, not actual future exam questions.
